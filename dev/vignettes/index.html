Vignettes

The vignettes are Quarto documents (Julia engine) in the vignettes/ directory of the repository, each in its own folder. They are rendered by the documentation workflow and bundled into this site; the links below open the rendered pages. To render them locally:

cd vignettes
julia --project=. -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.develop(path=".."); Pkg.instantiate()'
quarto render
